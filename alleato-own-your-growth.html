How it works

The method, at a glance

Broad goals like "get better at construction" don't move the needle. Precise ones do. Here's the whole loop — no download needed.

01 / OWN IT

Own It

Nobody cares about your development more than you. You lead this, not your manager. Open your list, add, remove, or rename until it's the real work you do.

02 / HONEST

Be Brutally Honest

You can only fix a weakness you're willing to name. Score each skill against the rubric — the exact number, not a rounded-up guess.

03 / SPECIFIC

Get Specific

"Read drawings better" is a wish. "30 min/day on civil sheets, RFI one thing per job" is a plan. Break each score into a precise, repeatable rep.

04 / FOCUS

Focus Your Energy

Don't spread thin. Pick a few — where focus goes, energy flows. Your top 2–4 gaps become the target. Re-score on a cadence and watch the wheel fill.

Quick toolkit

1·3·1

The 1-3-1 Rule

When you're stuck, bring one problem, three possible solutions, and your one recommendation. Turns "I'm stuck" into leadership.

↗

The Learning Ladder

1) Try it yourself → 2) Look it up (YouTube, AI, Claude, the drawings, the field) → 3) Bring a specific question → 4) Get coached → 5) Teach someone else.

✕

The "Stop-Doing" List

Skill-building needs time. Use the Eisenhower Matrix: important-not-urgent gets scheduled, urgent-not-important gets delegated, the rest gets dropped.

◎

Mentor Match

For each focus skill, name someone at Alleato near 100% at it — and book a 15-minute "sharper question" session.

Proficiency before promotion

What "ready" looks like

Advancement at Alleato follows capability, not tenure. Use this to see the reps between here and the next role.

✓

You've mastered the core skills of your current role — mostly 80%+ on your wheel.

✓

You run your work solo, document decisions, and escalate with a recommendation — not just a problem.

✓

You've done the reps on real projects (field / superintendent time counts) — not just watched.

✓

You make people around you better and can teach "the Alleato way."
