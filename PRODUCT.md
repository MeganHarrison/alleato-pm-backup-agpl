# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Alleato is used by construction project teams working inside active delivery workflows. Primary users include project managers coordinating scope, schedule, and commitments; executives and owners reviewing project risk and status; accounting and operations staff managing financial workflow accuracy; and admins or support staff maintaining project data quality. These users are typically operating under time pressure, switching between records, and validating decisions against source evidence.

## Product Purpose

Alleato is a construction project management platform modeled on Procore-class workflows, with stronger emphasis on clarity, actionability, and source-backed decision-making. The product helps teams manage project execution across tasks, budgets, commitments, change management, invoices, meetings, documents, scheduling, and AI-assisted operating workflows. Success means users can understand the current state quickly, take the next correct action without friction, and trust that surfaced insights connect back to the underlying project record or source material.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, operator-grade.

The interface should feel calm under pressure, technically credible, and built for serious work. It should communicate confidence through clarity rather than decoration. Copy should be direct, specific, and low-drama. Success states stay quiet. Failures should be explicit, actionable, and easy to recover from.

## Anti-references

- Procore-style clutter, overloaded chrome, gray-on-gray enterprise noise, and fragmented workflows.
- Generic admin-template dashboards with stat-card rows, filler summaries, and interchangeable SaaS UI patterns.
- Playful consumer-product styling, decorative illustrations, bright gamified feedback, or interfaces that optimize for charm over operator trust.
- One-off feature styling that diverges from shared app patterns without a workflow reason.

## Design Principles

- Design for execution: every surface should help the user decide, act, or verify, not just read.
- Reduce before adding: remove noise, duplicate content, and decorative structure before introducing new UI.
- Evidence earns trust: important claims, extracted insights, and workflow recommendations should stay close to their source records or canonical actions.
- Consistency compounds: repeated workflows should share layout, density, interaction patterns, and component vocabulary across the app.
- Fail loudly and recover clearly: errors, missing capabilities, and blocked states should be explicit, specific, and paired with the next useful action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Alleato should meet WCAG AA expectations for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, readable type, and predictable interaction patterns. Reduced motion preferences must be respected. Dense workflows should still maintain strong hierarchy, legible text, and touch-friendly interaction on smaller screens. Accessibility should support real operator use under fatigue, time pressure, and high information density rather than serving as a cosmetic compliance layer.
