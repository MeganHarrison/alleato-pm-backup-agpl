How it works

The Growth Cycle

Growth starts with the desire to improve and the willingness to take responsibility for the work. The Skill Wheel helps you understand where you are today, decide what matters next, and turn improvement into consistent action.

  1. 01

    Take ownership

    Choose to lead your development. Your manager can provide feedback and support, but you own the assessment, the actions, and the follow-through.

  2. 02

    Choose eight role-critical skills

    Identify the eight skills that matter most in your current role. These become the foundation of your Skill Wheel.

  3. 03

    Assess yourself honestly

    Use the proficiency scale and examples from your actual work to rate your current level. The goal is an accurate starting point, not a perfect score.

  4. 04

    Choose your focus

    Select two or three skills that would make the greatest difference in your work. Importance, current responsibilities, and potential impact should guide the decision.

  5. 05

    Define precise actions

    Decide exactly what you will practice, how often you will practice it, and what evidence will demonstrate improvement.

  6. 06

    Monitor and adjust

    Track your actions and results. Review progress with your manager and reassess at 30, 60, and 90 days. Keep what works and adjust what does not.
