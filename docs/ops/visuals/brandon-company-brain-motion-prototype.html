↓ why this matters

Not an AI answer. A decision surface.

Make the hidden chain of project context visible.

The visual is not the product goal. It is the fastest way to make source-backed reasoning understandable, inspectable, and easy to act on.

01 / Collect

Signals arrive as ordinary project work.

Meeting language, revised drawings, commitments, tasks, and correspondence are already there. The Brain gives them a shared context without asking the team to manage another system.

02 / Connect

The relationship, not the document count, carries the value.

When two pieces of evidence point to the same commercial consequence, the system can show its work instead of asking the user to trust an opaque answer.

03 / Act

Insight becomes a native workflow, while the proof stays close.

The ending is never “the AI found something.” The ending is a correct record, owner, follow-up, or decision that can be traced back later.

The buildable promiseEvery node is an existing record owner.
