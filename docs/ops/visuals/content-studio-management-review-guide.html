Recommended operating rhythm

A short routine prevents a large cleanup.

Weekly / Content owner

Clear the action queue.

  • Open the saved view for Needs attention.
  • Assign missing owners and reviewers.
  • Resolve items marked Never reviewed or Review due.
  • Move misplaced items with Displayed in.
  • Review items waiting In review.

Monthly / Executive review

Confirm the system is useful.

  • Scan all four destination counts for unexpected growth.
  • Review published content with no activity.
  • Separate Not tracked from truly unused content.
  • Archive obsolete or duplicative material.
  • Confirm important training has an accountable owner.

Before you publish

The six-point content quality check.

  • The title says exactly what the employee will learn or use.
  • The content type matches the job: course, guide, SOP, or resource.
  • Displayed in points to the correct employee destination.
  • An owner and an independent reviewer are assigned.
  • The content opens correctly and contains no stale instructions.
  • A next review date is set before the status becomes Published.

Common questions

When something does not look right.

Should I create a new item or move the existing one?

Move the existing item by changing Displayed in. Create a new item only when the content itself is meaningfully different. The catalog is designed to prevent parallel copies.

Does Not tracked mean nobody used the content?

No. It means that content's reader does not currently emit durable usage events. No activity is the zero-use state for content whose engagement can be measured.

What should I do if a bulk update fails?

Read the specific error, confirm the selected field, value, and your administrator access, then retry. The update is atomic, so a failed request does not partially change the selected catalog items.

Who should own and review content?

The owner is accountable for keeping the content current. The reviewer is the independent person qualified to approve accuracy. Avoid assigning both roles casually just to clear a warning.
