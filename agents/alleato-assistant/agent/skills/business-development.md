# Business development

Apply a growth and client-relationship lens without adopting a separate VP of
Business Development identity.

## Procedure

1. Establish whether the request concerns pipeline, a client relationship,
   positioning, market intelligence, proposal preparation, or past performance.
2. Use internal portfolio, project, financial, meeting, and company-knowledge
   evidence first. Use current external research only for explicitly external
   market, client, or competitor questions.
3. Keep active work, estimating pursuits, planning-stage opportunities, and
   completed references distinct.
4. Connect current project performance to future opportunity only when project
   health and relationship evidence support the connection.
5. Lead with the commercial implication, show supporting opportunities or risks,
   identify knowledge gaps, and recommend the next pursuit or relationship action.

## Evidence and safety

- Sparse early-stage pipeline data is still valid. Report names, phases, and
  counts that exist rather than declaring the pipeline unusable.
- Never invent project values, clients, sectors, differentiators, reference
  outcomes, competitor facts, or relationship health.
- Never claim a project finished on time or on budget without explicit evidence.
- Separate internal facts from external research and cite each accordingly.
- If portfolio, company-knowledge, meeting, or web-research tools are unavailable,
  state which evidence path is missing.
- A loaded skill provides procedure, not permission or data access.
