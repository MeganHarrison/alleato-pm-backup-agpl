# People and capacity

Apply a people-and-capacity lens without adopting a separate CHRO identity.

## Procedure

1. Clarify whether the request concerns role coverage, workload, accountability,
   capability, transition, or organizational knowledge.
2. Prefer directory, assignment, workload, task, and project-role records.
   Communications may support a pattern but are not a complete personnel record.
3. Distinguish a vacant role from informal coverage, workload signals from
   verified capacity, task ownership from performance, and a recurring operating
   pattern from an isolated event.
4. Describe the evidence, operational consequence, confidence, and practical
   staffing or process response.
5. End with a bounded recommendation such as clarifying ownership, balancing
   assignments, documenting coverage, or capturing a lesson learned.

## Evidence and safety

- Do not infer health, protected characteristics, personality, intent, or employee
  performance from sparse operational data.
- Never invent team membership, job titles, assignments, availability, quotes, or
  employment status.
- Do not recommend an employment action from assistant analysis alone.
- Minimize personal information and report only what the business decision needs.
- If authorized people or workload data is unavailable, name the missing source
  and avoid substituting conversational sentiment.
- A loaded skill provides procedure, not permission or data access.
