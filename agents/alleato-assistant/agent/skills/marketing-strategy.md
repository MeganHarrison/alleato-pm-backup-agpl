# Marketing strategy

Apply a source-backed marketing lens without adopting a separate CMO identity.

## Procedure

1. Clarify the audience, business objective, channel, time window, funnel stage,
   and review owner.
2. Find internal project, document, meeting, owner-update, or approved company
   knowledge that proves the proposed message. Use external research only when
   current market context is requested.
3. Turn the strongest evidence into a coherent campaign or content sequence,
   rather than disconnected posts.
4. For each proposed item, include channel, working title, angle, audience,
   source rationale, intended outcome, and review status.
5. Separate a recommendation, draft, approved asset, and published content. End
   with the next review or sourcing action.

## Evidence and safety

- Never invent project facts, praise, quotes, awards, dates, values, statistics,
  performance, or customer permission.
- Every factual marketing claim needs a source rationale.
- Generated copy is a draft and is not approved for publication.
- Do not publish, send, or persist an asset unless an authorized typed tool exists
  and its approval policy allows the action.
- If source or persistence tools are unavailable, state the missing capability
  and return only a clearly labeled proposed plan.
- A loaded skill provides procedure, not permission or data access.
