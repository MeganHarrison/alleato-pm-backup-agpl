# AI Assistant Actions and Current Limits

Eve is source-backed and read-capable across approved project and company data.
In the main AI Assistant, Eve can also create an RFI through a governed action.
Ask Alleato remains read-only.

## What Eve Can Read

- Project, portfolio, budget, cost, commitment, change, schedule, RFI, and
  submittal data available to your account.
- Authorized meetings, emails, Teams messages, documents, company knowledge,
  past conversations, and user memories.
- Project-intelligence packets and the current executive brief.
- Application help and the correct page for workflows that are not available
  as chat actions.

## Governed RFI Creation

When you ask Eve to create an RFI in the AI Assistant:

1. Eve collects the project, subject, question, due date, responsible party,
   and cost and schedule impacts.
2. The chat shows the exact action payload and asks for approval.
3. Denying or cancelling performs no write.
4. Approval resumes the same durable Eve call.
5. The app rechecks your current project and RFI write permission.
6. The app creates the RFI once and returns an idempotent execution receipt.

The approved business payload cannot set its own confirmation flag,
idempotency key, user, project permission, session, or tool-call identity.
Those controls are owned by Eve and the application boundary.

## Actions That Remain Unavailable

Other typed implementations in the codebase, including change events, change
orders, commitments, contacts, tasks, submittals, daily reports, messages,
calendar delivery, and payments, are not exposed through Eve yet. Their
presence in source code or in reusable UI does not mean they are live.

For those workflows, ask Eve to find or explain the correct page, then complete
the action in the normal authenticated application form.

## Safety and Failure Behavior

- Writes are never available in Ask Alleato.
- Eve does not advertise RFI creation without current RFI write permission.
- Unsigned, expired, tampered, cross-project, and cross-surface requests fail
  before execution.
- A retry or reconnect reuses the same action receipt instead of creating a
  second RFI.
- If the governed boundary is unavailable, Eve reports the failure rather than
  claiming the record was created.
