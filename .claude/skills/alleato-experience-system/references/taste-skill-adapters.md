# Taste Skill Adapters

`gpt-taste`, `design-taste-frontend`, and `design-taste-frontend-v1` are global expressive-frontend skills. They are not Alleato product authorities.

## Routing firewall

Classify the surface before borrowing any guidance:

- **Alleato product surface:** application shell, resource library, table, form, dashboard, workbench, object detail, AI workflow, admin, or authenticated tool. Use the Alleato Experience System. Do not invoke the taste skills as generators.
- **Alleato brand surface:** campaign, public landing page, event, editorial story, recruiting page, or portfolio-like artifact where visual expression is itself part of the product. `design-taste-frontend` v2 may be consulted after Alleato brand and accessibility constraints are established.
- **Cinematic/Awwwards experiment:** use `gpt-taste` only when the user explicitly asks for a motion-led promotional experiment and accepts its performance and accessibility tradeoffs.

The user saying "premium," "modern," or "not boring" does not change a product surface into a brand surface.

## `design-taste-frontend` v2

### Incorporate

- infer the brief from page kind, audience, references, brand assets, and quiet constraints;
- state a concise design read;
- distinguish preserve, overhaul, and greenfield redesign modes;
- audit current brand tokens, information architecture, content, behavior, and accessibility before changing;
- verify dependencies before importing libraries;
- lock palette, shape vocabulary, theme, icon family, and CTA terminology;
- audit repeated layout families, copy quality, button contrast, mobile collapse, reduced motion, performance, and real asset requirements;
- use real design systems honestly rather than approximating them from memory.

### Adapt

- use expressiveness, motion, and density as contextual calibration dials without its global `8 / 6 / 4` baseline;
- treat its anti-tells as prompts for inspection, not universal bans;
- apply hero, image, AIDA, and marketing-section guidance only to brand surfaces;
- preserve current route, analytics, content, and interaction contracts during redesign unless change is authorized.

### Reject for product UI

- mandatory real images on every minimal surface;
- replacing long operational lists with carousels, marquees, pills, or card grids;
- blanket bans on Inter, eyebrows, dividers, or standard product patterns;
- mandatory dual-theme work without a product requirement;
- high expressiveness and motion as defaults;
- invented "organic" data or names;
- a 1,200-line preflight that dilutes the few gates that matter.

The skill is over 12,000 words and exceeds the recommended 500-line skill body. Its block-library paths are aspirational and do not provide the promised implementations. Use selected principles, not the whole file.

## `design-taste-frontend-v1`

The v1 install is a backward-compatibility artifact with the same broad trigger territory as v2.

### Incorporate

- dependency verification;
- explicit loading, empty, error, and interaction states;
- transform/opacity animation discipline;
- cleanup, responsive collapse, and reduced-motion concerns.

### Reject

- fixed `8 / 6 / 4` creative defaults;
- perpetual animation, magnetic controls, liquid glass, and bento as routine UI;
- enormous radii and gallery-style cards as a universal premium formula;
- exact icon/font mandates that override the repository;
- forcing motion and asymmetry when the job needs conventional product interaction.

Keep v1 out of automatic discovery for new work. Retain it only for a named legacy dependency.

## `gpt-taste`

`gpt-taste` is an Awwwards marketing generator, not a general UX/UI skill.

### Narrowly useful

- keep marketing heroes concise and legible;
- verify button contrast;
- avoid empty bento cells;
- limit repeated cards;
- use professional assets;
- prevent accidental horizontal overflow.

### Reject for Alleato product work

- simulated Python randomization;
- mandatory AIDA page structure;
- forced navigation, hero, bento, desire chapter, CTA, and footer;
- static-interface prohibition;
- mandatory GSAP, pinning, scrub, stack, and hover scaling;
- massive section spacing;
- ambient glow, mesh, grain, and image effects as defaults;
- Picsum placeholders as a production asset strategy.

Randomness creates novelty, not taste. Alleato coherence must come from calibrated intent, domain workflow, canonical patterns, and rendered critique.

## Conflict rule

When any taste skill conflicts with `AGENTS.md`, the live repository, the Alleato Experience System, the product noise gate, usability, accessibility, or performance, the taste skill loses.
